About Terrahide Global

Built on trust, tanned in tradition

We connect world-class tanneries with global markets — giving furniture makers, fashion houses, and automotive suppliers direct access to premium leather without the friction of cross-border sourcing.

Global HQ Network

Sydney · Melbourne · São Paulo

◈

Mission

To make world-class leather sourcing simple, transparent, and reliable for makers everywhere.

◈

Vision

A global leather trade that is fully traceable — from hide to finished product — by design, not exception.

◈

Values

Craftsmanship, integrity, and long-term partnership over short-term margin.

Our journey

1998

First tannery partnership formed

Our founders began sourcing upholstery hides from a single family-run tannery in South America.

2007

Expansion into craft & belting leather

New tannery partners in Europe brought vegetable-tanned leather into the catalogue.

2015

Regional warehousing network launched

Local stockholding across key markets cut lead times from months to days.

2026

Terrahide Global — 18 tanneries, 42 countries

Today we operate as a full-service global sourcing and distribution partner.
