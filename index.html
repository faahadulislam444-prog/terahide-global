The firmest, most uniform fibre on the whole hide. Minimal stretch in any direction, which is why it carries load without deforming.

A Better Planet

Environment & Sustainability

Leather is a by-product of the meat and dairy industry — a natural, biodegradable material far removed from petroleum-based synthetics. Terrahide Global only partners with tanneries certified for responsible water treatment, chrome-free tanning options, and traceable hide origin.

Every year, the leather industry diverts over 73 million tonnes of by-product from global landfill through environmentally aware regulation and reuse.

Why Terrahide

What a sourcing partner should actually do

We audit before we buy

Every tannery passes a four-stage audit — site inspection, certification review, trial batch, and annual re-audit — before a single hide enters our network.

We hold stock locally

Regional warehousing across four continents means stocked grades ship in days, not months — and freight emissions stay down.

Every hide is traceable

Give us a batch number and we'll return the tannery, the region, and the certification documents behind it. No exceptions.

One team, start to finish

The person who quotes your order is the person who follows it through production, QC, and delivery. No handoffs, no ticket queues.
